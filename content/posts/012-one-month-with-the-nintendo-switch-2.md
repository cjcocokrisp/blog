Hello everyone, another gaming related article today. A little over a month ago, I finally
purchased a Nintendo Switch 2. I didn't get one at launch because none of the games really
stood out to me and all the current releases still really don't. However, I said I would buy
the console when a new Fire Emblem was releasing and it's coming out in September. The price
increases were coming as well at that time so I decided to pick one up now to play through some
of the routes I didn't play in Fire Emblem Three Houses before Fortune's Weave drops. My 20177
Switch is on its last legs so I new one was really needed. This article is going to be covering 
my thoughts on the console so far and what I've been up to with it.

## Summed Up Thoughts

Before I begin, as always I'll do my TLDR of my review. Overall, I think that hardware is for sure
a step up from the original Switch. Even though I think the new software library is incredibly
weak at the moment this console is perfect for people who's original Switch is on its last legs
and needs to upgrade. It's really nice to see Nintendo actually care about hardware specs and make
something thats pretty powerful.

## Hardware

First big section I want to talk about is the hardware. The Switch 2's form factor is amazing.
It's not too small but its also not too big. It's still really portable and is really comfortable
to use over long play session in handheld mode. The screen on it is such a step up from the original
Switch. I like the screen on this a lot better then the Steam Deck. I think I still enjoy the form
factor of the Steam Deck a lot more but its still really solid here.

The entire build of the system has just been improved ten fold. The kickstand is actually nice now.
It has a ton of different viewing angles and is actually sturdy unlike the original Switch. I'm
pretty sure that was kind of fixed by the OLED though. Even the game card slot covering feels more
secure. Having USB C ports for charging on both the top and bottom is also a huge improvement.

The battery life has also been really good. Only game I've played in really long sessions in
handheld is Fire Emblem Three Houses and I got around 4 hours of battery life playing that game. With
the Switch 1 I would only get about 2 to 2 and a half so its improved for sure. I played it on a
long car ride that I played it on before back on my original Switch I started the drive at 100 and
ended on 10 but this time started on 100 and ended around 50 so that was funny to see.

The dock for the system is also nice. Kind of unfortunate they removed the USB port in the back and
replaced it with Ethernet but thats fine the rumble USB is not needed for the Gamecube Adaptor.
Not really much more to say about the doc.

The system specs are also a lot more powerful here with Switch 1 games loading a lot quicker. It's
crazy playing Smash Bros Ultimate on this thing. The character select screen renders load instantly.
I haven't played a game that got a Switch 2 edition yet but I will be soon so I hope that will be
even more crazy.

## Controllers

The new Joy Cons are such a step up from the original. The original Switch's Joy Cons are not bad
by any means. They do have their issues like lots of stick drift but they are not the end of the
world. They are useable controllers and thats what matters. However, I would still always prefer 
a Pro Controller over them. With the Joy Cons 2s I don't think I would still have that opinion. 

The buttons on the Switch 2 Joy Cons feel pretty better. They are a bit larger too so they don't
feel tiny and best of all for single Joy Con use the buttons on the back are larger and not beyond
tiny. If I was still at University I would be bringing this to play games with friends in between
classes all the time with single Joy Con.

One thing that surprised me the most was how the Joy Cons actually connect to the console. I thought
that they would feel too wobbly with them being magnetic but nope they are a lot more stable and probably
will last longer then the Switch 1's Joy Cons. The rails on my 2017 Switch started to get really beat up
after the years of using it so hopefully this one lasts longer.

I did pick up a new Pro Controller to use with my system. I debated getting the Switch 2 Pro Controller but
ended up getting the Xenoblade 2 Controller. I've wanted this Pro Controller for awhile so it was nice to finally
get one. I would have just used the one that I had for my Switch 1 but that one started getting drift.

## Software

Next up I want to talk about the software on the system. It is pretty much the same as the Switch 1 with an overhauled look and
the Game Chat feature. I have not been able to try out the Game Chat feature but I can't see it being that useful when Discord exists
and its always on my second monitor. The new look of the UI looks really nice. I thought it was underwhelming at first but after using
the system it for sure looks better. I'm also a big fan of all the new icons they added. I'm rocking the Oshawott one because that's
my favorite Pokemon and it's probably the one I will be rocking for the whole time I have the system. On my original Switch I used my
Mii the whole time so I don't usually change it often.

Being able to transfer everything over from your Switch 1 was also nice. I decided to start fresh however and just signed into my
account. I have so much random garbage on my 20127 Switch that I just don't use so I wanted it to be more minimal here. 

I do have one complaint about the software. The virtual game cards are kind of annoying. I understand it for full blown games but
DLCs count as well. So for example when I booted my physical copy of Smash Ultimate on the Switch 2 I expected the DLC to just be
there. However, it wasn't and I had to go on my Switch 1 and eject the game card for it. I want to Switch between the two to play
with mods on Smash for the less online delay mod but I haven't done that cause having to switch every time would be annoying.

## What I've Been Playing

For the last section I want to talk about what I've been playing on the system. As you can see in the cover photo there's four games
there that I have played being Link's Awakening, Fire Emblem Three Houses, Super Monkey Ball Banana Mania, and Smash Ultimate.

Fire Emblem Three Houses is the first game that I played through and completed on the system. I played through the Crimson Flower
route on New Game Maddening which was a lot of fun. It was hard but I had a good time playing through it. I only played the Silver
Snow route back when it came out so I do want to get back to the other 2 routes + the DLC which I own but haven't played. Playing
the routes back to back seemed pretty draining so I started playing something else to not burn myself out of Fire Emblem before
Fortune's Weave.

Next, Monkey Ball Banana Mania. This is my frist Monkey Ball game and I got it for free at a local game store. Before getting the
console I went to this local game store and traded in a few Switch games I never was going to play again. I got Link's Awakening
and Xenoblade Chronicles Definitive Edition for $50. The store does buy two get one free and Monkey Ball was the free game that I got
so that was pretty cool. Monkey Ball has always been on my radar I just never tried it out. This game has been really fun and I've
only been playing it on the train during my commutes to work. Its the perfect game for that. There has been some really challenging
levels so I'm surprised.

Finally, is Link's Awakening (I'm skipping Smash cause this is not really a new game I've been playing this since launch). This is
the game I'm currently playing through. It's been a blast so far and I'm done with six of the eight dungeons. I love the linear style
of Zelda so I hope they keep doing linear Zeldas instead of just open world like BOTW and TOTK.

## Conclusion

So anyway, that's my thoughts on the Nintendo Switch 2 so far. It's been a really fun console to use and I'm really looking forward
to seeing what games they're cooking up for it. Fire Emblem Fortunte's Weave will be the first Switch 2 exclusive that I play so I'm
excited to see the console's specs get pushed to the limits there. Anyway, that's really all I have to say about the console so I will
see you guys in the next one.
