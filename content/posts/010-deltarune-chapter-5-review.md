**THIS REVIEW WILL CONTAIN SPOILERS FOR DELTARUNE CHAPTER 5**

*Quick review for those that want to stay spoiler free:*

*Deltarune Chapter 5 has a lot more chill of a tone compared to the prior chapter. I don't think that's a bad thing though.
The chapter has amazing moments, great characters, awesome gameplay gimmicks, and overall just tons of personality. As
Toby Fox said, "Let’s turn around and watch the sun, before it goes down completely. Let’s have one more fun adventure, okay?"
Those words are more then true and if you enjoyed the other chapters you will defintely enjoy this one too! The weird route will also
probably make you regret ever doing it...*

Deltarune Chapter 5 released this past week and over the last couple of days I've spent a majority of my free time playing through it.
Last year, I played through the entire game when Chapters 3 and 4 released. I played Chapters 1 and 2 when they released but that was
so long ago that I forgot a lot about them. Because my blog wasn't around when those released here's my quick thoughts on the rest of
the game so far. Chapter 4 was pretty much my favorite and the rest of the game I very much enjoyed. One thing I really like about the
release of Deltarune is how the time between chapters lets you really notice the hidden details and start theorizing about whats going
to happen. When I was younger I was really into theories with other games so if this came out when I was younger this probably would
be my favorite game ever. As usual, I'll be breaking down the review into sections about various aspects of the chapter.

## Gameplay

Chapter 5 has what is probably the best gameplay in the entire game. Starting with the combat, there were so many good fights in this
chapter and Toby really cooked with the different actions you can take to spare enemies. There were a couple of fights where items
were attached to the soul and you had to use them to do various things to progress it. For example, one enemy has a watering can
attached to the soul and when you stand still water drops out. Fill all the pots below the box and you will spare the enemy. There
was another enemy that I really liked their spare gimmick and it was to figure out which of their bullets are fake and then touch
those fake bullets. Just really cool fight mechanics overall.

The boss fights in this chapter were also really good. The bosses really fit well with the characters that you were fighting which
I'll talk about later. My favorite boss in the chapter had a detective style minigame in it where you had to figure out who did the
crime to progress the fight. The final boss of the chapter introduced the orange soul as well. In this mode if you hold down the
confirm button it will charge up a dash which you can use to break through things and also hit the boss as he tries to hit you in
the box. The secret boss of the chapter was also really fun. Its basically a genocide version of the Muffet fight from Undertale so that was really
fun. It also had a date sim element in it that you needed to progress the fight with a unique gimmick where the boss's personality
splits into two and you need to pick an option that answers both of their questions.

The final section of gameplay I want to talk about is the feather. This chapter has a gimmick where once you unlock the feather you
can activate these statues and the game will turn into a platformer. This was really fun because it mixed up the gameplay while not
taking over the whole chapter there was still normal fights but there were also platformer fights that you had to do as well. There
were some fun challenges here and it was a good change of pace. The puzzles in this chapter were also very good.

## Story

The story in this chapter was pretty good. The chapter starts out with the festival and some amazing moments between Susie and Noelle.
The lead up to the dark world as Kris was opening up the dark fountain was also really tense because Susie was nearby. Once entering
the dark world the main antagonist of the dark world Flowery shows up with probably the most unique intro in the entire game which was
an anime styled cutscene and this really caught me off guard. His whole goal is to give Asgore a world that he can be the king in. It
was cool to see him in a varient of his Undertale armor in this chapter as well. You continue through the dark world just trying to
close it. The theming of this dark world is very heavily based on cherry blossoms and flowers which ended up looking really cool.
My favorite locations in the chapter had to be when you were climbing the mountain. At the end of the chapter the Roaring Knight
shows up as usual and its a pretty intense moment. After that Susie comes to the realization that they need more help to deal with all
that's going on so they begin to prep to bring Noelle into the dark worlds again which is whats going to happen in Chapter 6. Overall,
the plot was good and I enjoyed it a lot. Definitely a lot more chill then Chapter 4 but that's ok I think it was done really well.
There were also a lot of insane jokes in this chapter. There were a few text boxes in Castle Town references Umamusume and the Roblox
game Dress to Impress.

## Characters

This was the highlight of the chapter to me, Flowery and all of the flower people were just all highlights. They were all goofy and
interacted really well. My favorite of the flower people is either Aqua or Green I really can't decide they both had awesome moments.
Later in the chapter you can also take breaks and do these little dialogue sequences with the flower people and it adds a lot to their
characters. Susie's development really shined in this chapter too you can really see how far she's come since the beginning of the
game which was nice. Ralsei also has a really interesting dynamic with Flowery and you get to see a side of Ralsei we haven't got to
before. The Fun gang is just a really awesome party.

## Music

As we know with Toby Fox he makes amazing music. This chapter featured a lot of tracks that he had people collab with. Here are my
top 3 tracks from the chapter.

1. [Sunset of Seven Suns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L4pNR0UJBnk)
2. [Flower Man](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWf9_qSrnOM)
3. [Who might you be?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQsr0_RUMJU)

One thing I really liked about the final boss of the chapter's track Flower Man is during the boss fight there's some lyrics on the
screen that aren't actually sang like Raise Your Bat in Chapter 3. People are already making covers with the lyrics and that's just so
cool to see.

It's not in my top three but the [secret boss track](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ii81enXEL6g) actually does have lyrics sung 
and they are a vocaloid. I looked up the vocaloid and it's Hatsune Miku. Hatsune Miku singing over the freedom motif was not something
that I had in my predictions at all for this game but here we are.

## Weird Route

The weird route in this chapter was insane. It's really short only taking like 20 minutes to beat the chapter but when it ends it 
has this black screen with the text "Insert Chapter 7 Side B." So I guess that Chapter 6 is skipped in the weird route which is so
insane. The official name of the route is confirmed to just be Side B, I think I'll keep calling it weird route though. It also 
does not show up as completion but a save file does get created in the files of the game.

## Final Thoughts

Overall, I really enjoyed this chapter. It's probably going to be the last silly chapter that we have for this game and the rest of it
is going to be super serious and probably crush all of our hearts. I'm really looking forward to Chapter 6 and 7 so we'll just need
to wait till next year at least 6 will be released. I would say this chapter is probably my second favorite in the game. Thanks for
reading and I will see you all in the next one which will hopefully be tech related. I have some things brewing for that but I've
just been busy so I haven't had much times on weekends to work on projects.
