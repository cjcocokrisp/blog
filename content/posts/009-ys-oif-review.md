**THIS REVIEW WILL CONTAIN SPOILERS FOR YS: THE OATH IN FELGHANA**


*Quick review for those that want to stay spoiler free:*

*YS: The Oath in Felghana is a remake of Ys III: Wanderers from Ys and is a really amazing game. The characters
and story are interesting, the combat and exploration are really fun, and the biggest highlight of the game for me
is the bosses. I played the game on hard and each boss presented a challenge. None of them felt unfair or impossible
was just challenging. The game is not incredibly long and I would highly recommend it to anyone who enjoys action
RPGs or wants to check out the Ys series. Easily a 10/10 from me.*

After completing Ys I+II Chronicles+, I decided to continue the streak of playing Ys games with Oath in Felghana. 
As I mentioned in my review for that post I picked up Oath in Felghana and Ark of Napishtim. I decided to play
OiF first because it is a remake of Ys III. I really enjoyed this game if you did not get that from the quick review.
It was really challenging (I played on hard) but overall nothing felt impossible and it was fun.

A bit about the original before I give my review. The original game released in that weird time where 
the sequel (even though its the third game because the first two are parts of a whole) would change the 
gameplay up entirely. Ys got the Zelda treatment where the sequel was a 2D platformer. The original Ys III was apparently
so bad it almost killed the series in the west. Like I mentioned in my review of I+II Chronicles. It was fun to go back
and see how certain parts of the game looked in the originals. In this case it was a lot more jarring because it 
was a whole different gameplay style.

## Gameplay

First up, I want to talk about the gameplay. First the combat, you have simple attack strings that you can do
along with aerial attacks and magic. The attack string is 5 hits and then there's a brief pause before you can start again.
The aerial attacks have a 3 hit string you can do while rising, a 360 spin as you're rising if you're standing still, and
a downwards strike that can stun enemies as you're falling. These basic attacks feel incredibly smooth and comboing enemies
is really fun. You also get three magic attacks as you proceed through the game. The first is a fireball, then a wind spin,
and finally a forward charge that you're invincible while moving. The magic also acted as movement options too.
Finally for the last part of combat is the burst that you get. It charges overtime and once fully charged you can 
activate it and while its active you take less damage and can swing a lot faster then normal allowing you to do a 
ton of damage. Even though on paper the combat seems simple, it feels really good and fast paced.

For exploratin and areas, a lot of the areas had alternate paths and things that would only unlock as you
got more magic spells and it was fun to go back and say I probably could get over that pit now or break through that wall.
The dungeons all felt really unique and even though this is not a 2D platformer like the original they kept some platforming
sections. It was fun to be trying to jump around and do platforming while beating up enemies. All the enemies were fair and
it didn't feel like any of them were too BS. Some of the areas were pretty challenging too. One thing that made Ys I+II a
bit easier was the fact that you could save anywhere. In this game you cannot do that so you need to be careful not to die
and it actually makes dying mean something which was nice.

The progression in the game was good as well. All of your equipment could be leveled up twice and each magic spell
had jewels that would enhance their effects. The first one would enable a charge attack and the other two just made mana
recover quicker and damage higher. This game also had elixirs you could find that would raise your max HP a bit. So those
were pretty fun to find.

## Graphics & Art Style

Oath in Felghana orginally released in 2005 so this game has that 2000s anime art style that I love. A rerelease of this game
came out on modern consoles (Steam version is still the one from 2005 it just got the voice acting ported over) and the
portraits in that one don't look as good in my opinion. Only plus I saw from the art of that game is one of the characters
Elena dressing up as other Ys characters with one of them being Laxia from Ys VIII so it was cool to see that reference. 
There's just something about the 2000s anime style but I really need to experience more media with it.

The actual graphics of the game were great. I was suprised that the game also supports ultrawide monitors but I will not
complain. As this game came out a year after Trails in the Sky, some of the environments really reminded me of the game and
the sprites were back too so it was oddly nostalgic. I really like Falcom's graphics for this time so I'm excited to play
more games from them in this era.

## Plot & Characters

Next up the plot and characters. The plot was interesting and it progressed really well. The plot begins with Adol and Dogi
arriving in Felghana which is Dogi's home country. I was excited to get more backstory on Dogi here. You quickly learn that
Dogi left kind of unexpectedly and you meet his childhood friend Elena who has been searching for her brother Chester. It is
made clear from the start the Dogi, Elena, and Chester were all good childhood friends. Monsters have been showing up in 
Felghana recently so Adol begins to help out with things happening in Dogi's hometown and things start to progress from
there slowly building up to dealing with the true evil behind all of this. The plot was solid and a lot of the end game
moments had huge stakes to them and the ending was really bittersweet. 

The characters were also really good with Chester and Elena being the highlights. A lot of the other NPCs in town also were
great. Falcom is really good at writing NPCs and random townspeople so this game just continues to show that. One funny
thing is Chester is a really cool character but I can't get over his name being Chester its just a funny name.

## Final Thoughts

As you can tell I really really really enjoyed Oath in Felghana. This game is easily a 10/10 from me and I can't really
think of much about the game that I did not enjoy. It was fun, challenging, and had a good plot. I haven't platinumed the
game yet. Unfortunately the achievements in this game require some replays which I do plan on working through over the next
couple of weeks while playing through Deltarune Chapter 5.

I would easily recommend this game to anyone and for $5 on a Steam sale it's really a no brainer. Such a good game for such
a cheap price. I still think I like Ys VIII more but it's really close. This game was really solid. Anyway, that's my review
for Oath in Felghana and I hope this convinces you to try the game. Thanks for reading and see you all in the next one!
